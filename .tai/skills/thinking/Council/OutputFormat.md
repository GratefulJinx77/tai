# Output FormatStandard format for council debate transcripts.## Full Debate Transcript```markdown## Council Debate: [Topic]### Round 1: Initial Positions**Architect (Serena):**[Position from architectural perspective]**Designer (Aditi):**[Position from design perspective]**Engineer (Marcus):**[Position from implementation perspective]**Researcher (Ava):**[Position with data/precedent]### Round 2: Responses & Challenges**Architect (Serena):**[Responds to specific points from Round 1]**Designer (Aditi):**[Responds to specific points from Round 1][...]### Round 3: Synthesis**Architect (Serena):**[Final position, areas of agreement/disagreement][...]### Council Synthesis**Areas of Convergence:**- [Points where multiple agents agreed]**Remaining Disagreements:**- [Points still contested]**Recommended Path:**[Synthesized recommendation]```## Quick Council Format```markdown## Quick Council: [Topic]### Perspectives**Architect (Serena):**[Brief take - 30-50 words]**Designer (Aditi):**[Brief take][...]### Quick Summary**Consensus:** [Do they agree? On what?]**Concerns:** [Red flags raised?]**Recommendation:** [Proceed / Reconsider / Need full debate]```## Session Transcript Format```markdown## Council Session: [Topic]**Team:** council-{slug}**Members:** [List with teammate names]**Mode:** Persistent session (3 rounds + follow-up available)### Round 1: Initial Positions**🏛️ Architect (Serena):**[Position from architectural perspective]**🎨 Designer (Aditi):**[Position from design perspective]**⚙️ Engineer (Marcus):**[Position from implementation perspective]**🔍 Researcher (Ava):**[Position with data/precedent]### Round 2: Responses & Challenges[Same format as DEBATE Round 2]### Round 3: Synthesis[Same format as DEBATE Round 3]### Council Synthesis**Areas of Convergence:**- [Points where multiple agents agreed]**Remaining Disagreements:**- [Points still contested]**Recommended Path:**[Synthesized recommendation]**Open Questions:**1. [Another unresolved question]---**Session Active** | Team: council-{slug} | Members: {N} | Open Questions: {N}**Follow-up commands:**- "Bring back the council" — reconvene all members with full context- "Bring back [Name]" — recall one member for targeted discussion- "Council: [new question]" — new round with updated context- "Close the council session" — shutdown team and archive```## Session Follow-Up Format```markdown## Council Follow-Up: [Original Topic]**Context:** [User's follow-up question or new information]**Members Recalled:** [All / specific member name]**🏛️ Architect (Serena):**[Response building on prior position, referencing original debate][... other recalled members ...]### Updated Synthesis**New Convergence:**- [Any shifts in agreement]**Remaining Open Questions:**1. [Updated list]```## Session Closure Format```markdown---**Council Session Closed** | Topic: {topic} | Rounds: {N} | Duration: {time}**Final Open Questions** (if any):1. [question]Transcript archived at: `MEMORY/WORK/council-{slug}/transcript.md````## Output Requirements- **Length:** 50-150 words per agent per round (debate/session), 30-50 words (quick)- **Tone:** Professional but direct; genuine challenges- **Must Include:** Specific references to other agents' points in Round 2+- **Must Avoid:** Generic opinions, restating initial position without engagement- **SESSION-specific:** Follow-up responses must reference the original debate, not just restate fresh opinions
